## Brand & Style

This design system embodies medical minimalism and clinical purity tailored for a premier dental practice. It translates clinical precision into an environment of soothing reassurance, uncompromising hygiene, and boutique hospitality. The target demographic comprises discerning individuals, families, and international patients seeking state-of-the-art restorative, aesthetic, and surgical dental care.

The interface must evoke an immediate sense of physiological calm: lowering anxiety, projecting effortless expertise, and inspiring absolute confidence in sterilization and craft. To achieve this, the visual language balances pristine clinical clarity with ambient soft-glass depth. Surfaces remain airy and uncluttered, pairing the structural cleanliness of surgical environments with the tranquil warmth of high-end wellness spaces. Generous whitespace, precise typography, and soft luminous layering communicate pristine purity without sterile coldness.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of pristine clinical hygiene accented by restorative marine hues:

- **Primary (`#0D9488` - Deep Teal / Ocean Pine):** The anchor for primary calls-to-action, key state indicators, trusted verification badges, and focal brand moments. Conveys surgical precision, vitality, and grounded trust.
- **Secondary (`#F0F7FF` - Soft Ice Blue):** The primary tinted surface background. Used for subtle canvas differentiation, section breaks, and underlying wash layers to soften pure white glare.
- **Tertiary (`#CCFBF1` - Pale Aqua Wash):** An auxiliary tint utilized for soft interactive feedback, positive diagnostic alerts, status tags, and illuminated backdrop glows.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate):** High-contrast, optical-grade slate for typography and structural iconography, ensuring effortless legibility without the harshness of pitch black.

### Functional & Surface Application
- **Base Canvas:** Pure Medical White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational layer.
- **Subtle Surface Borders:** Ultra-refined slate outline (`#E2E8F0`) at low opacity ensures discrete structural containment without visual clutter.
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** Cool slate tint (`#64748B`) provides a secondary typographic tier for clinical details, opening hours, and ancillary notes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometry, soft modern terminals, and balanced readability. Its rounded clarity counterbalances technical medical terminology with an approachable, empathetic character.

- **Headlines & Displays:** Rendered in semi-bold to bold weights with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`). This maintains typographic density and authoritative structure across large treatments.
- **Body Text:** Kept light to normal weight with relaxed line-heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate smooth scanning of dental procedures, treatment plans, and post-operative guidance.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted with slightly tracked letter spacing and medium/semi-bold weights, ensuring appointment badges, time slots, and dosage notes remain legible at glance-level scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet screens, folding into a 4-column arrangement on mobile devices. Maximum layout container width is capped at 1280px to preserve comfortable scan lengths and maintain an architectural, editorial composition.

- **Grid Strategy:** Desktop utilizes `1.5rem` gutters with `2rem` screen margins. Handheld viewports drop to `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins to maximize edge-to-edge touch target real estate.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous outer margins and expansive vertical spacing separate procedural blocks, giving the patient cognitive breathing room between sections.
- **Reflow Rules:** Clinical service cards shift from 3-column rows on desktop (spanning 4 columns each) to 2-column formats on tablet, collapsing into single full-width tactile cards on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass surfaces and subtle ambient shadows rather than opaque heavy layering:

- **Frosted Glassmorphism:** Primary cards and floating navigational headers use translucent pure white backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) coupled with a hardware-accelerated `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. This exposes delicate gradients of Ice Blue (`#F0F7FF`) and soft teal glowing behind them.
- **Ghost Boundary Lines:** Every elevated glass container features a 1px border (`#E2E8F0` at 60% opacity) that defines clear bounds without heavy outlines.
- **Atmospheric Shadows:** Elevation utilizes low-opacity shadows tinted with slate and deep ocean tones:
  - *Tier 1 (Resting Cards):* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.02)`
  - *Tier 2 (Hover / Modals / Active Tooltips):* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.05)`
  - *Tier 3 (Floating Action Bars / Urgent Drawers):* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a `rounded-2xl` geometric signature (16px / `1rem`), blending clinical precision with biological softness.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Set precisely to `1rem` (`rounded-2xl`) to avoid harsh clinical edges while keeping layout structure orderly.
- **Nested Inner Modules:** Child components (e.g., date-time pickers, dental diagram selectors, input groups) scale down proportionally to `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- **Interactive Badges & Pills:** Appointment chips, status flags, and primary action triggers utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`) to create friendly, tactile targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Consultation / Confirm Treatment):** Solid `#0D9488` fill, white typography (`label-lg`), pill-shaped (`9999px`), 44px min-height. Hover shifts to `#0F766E` with subtle ambient teal glow. Active state scales gently (`0.98`).
- **Secondary (Learn More / View Credentials):** Semi-transparent Ice Blue background (`#F0F7FF`), `#0D9488` text, 1px border in `#CCFBF1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure transparent background, `#0F172A` text, underlined with primary teal on hover.

### Cards
- **Service & Doctor Profiles:** Frosted white substrate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), 16px corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and Tier 1 elevation. Hover gently elevates card to Tier 2 with a 1px border shift toward `#CCFBF1`.
- **Inner Padding:** 24px desktop, 16px mobile.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** 48px height, pristine white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px `#0D9488` stroke with an ambient ice-blue outer ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(204, 251, 241, 0.5)`). Placeholder set to `#94A3B8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded for checkboxes (`4px`), fully circular for radios. Inactive border `#CBD5E1`. Selected state fills with `#0D9488` showcasing a crisp white medical tick or dot.

### Chips & Selection Badges
- **Time-Slot / Treatment Filters:** Pill shape, 32px height, 12px padding. Unselected: `#F8FAFC` fill with subtle `#E2E8F0` border. Selected: `#0D9488` text with `#CCFBF1` background and `#0D9488` border.

### Odontogram / Interactive Tooth Chart
- Vector-rendered tooth surfaces using clinical white fills (`#FFFFFF`) with `#CBD5E1` strokes. Treated, watched, or healthy surfaces dynamically tint with `#0D9488` and `#CCFBF1` overlays.
